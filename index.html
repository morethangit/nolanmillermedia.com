About

Behind

I'm Nolan Miller — a director of photography and lighting designer based in Cincinnati, Ohio.

I'm currently studying at the University of Cincinnati. I shoot short films, music videos and branded content, and I design, program and operate lighting for concerts, worship services and live events.

The two feed each other. Designing a room teaches you how light behaves at scale; shooting teaches you how little of it you actually need.
